Understanding Your Diagnosis

You have been diagnosed with hypertension (high blood pressure). This means the force of blood pushing against the walls of your arteries is too high.

Your doctor has prescribed lisinopril, a medication that helps relax blood vessels. This makes it easier for your heart to pump blood.

You should take one tablet every morning. If you forget, take it as soon as you remember — but not if it is almost time for your next dose.
