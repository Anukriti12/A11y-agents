Your Rights at Work

Quick summary: You have the right to be paid fairly, to work in a safe place, and to not be treated badly because of who you are.

Your pay

Your boss must pay you at least the minimum wage. Right now that is $7.25 an hour. Some states pay more. Check your state's rules.

Your safety

Your workplace must be safe. If it is not safe, you can report it. Your boss cannot fire you for reporting a safety problem.

Fairness

No one can treat you badly because of your age, race, religion, or disability.
