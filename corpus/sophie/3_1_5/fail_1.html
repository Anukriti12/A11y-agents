Health Insurance Policy Terms and Conditions

The indemnification obligations hereunder shall not apply to the extent that the Policyholder's liability arises from the Policyholder's own gross negligence or willful misconduct. Notwithstanding any provision to the contrary, the aggregate liability of the Insurer under this Policy shall not exceed the Maximum Indemnity Amount specified in the Schedule of Benefits appended hereto.

Preauthorization requirements are applicable to all elective procedures and non-emergent inpatient hospitalizations. The Policyholder must obtain prior authorization from the Plan Administrator as delineated in the Utilization Management Protocol. Failure to obtain preauthorization may result in reduction or denial of benefits pursuant to Section 14(b)(iii) of this Policy.
