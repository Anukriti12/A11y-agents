How to Vote in Your Local Election

Voting is easy. Here are the steps to follow on election day.

  1. Go to your polling place. You can find it on your voter registration card.
  2. Show your ID to the person at the desk.
  3. They will give you a ballot.
  4. Go to a private booth. Mark your choices on the ballot.
  5. Put your ballot in the box.
  6. You have voted! You may get a sticker.

If you need help, ask a poll worker. They are there to help you.
