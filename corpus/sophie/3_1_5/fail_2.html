Study Participation Information Sheet

This randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial is designed to evaluate the pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic properties of the investigational medicinal product in a heterogeneous patient population stratified by comorbidity indices and baseline inflammatory biomarker profiles.

Participants will be required to provide informed consent prior to enrollment and will undergo periodic assessments including venipuncture for hematological analysis, urinalysis, and electrocardiographic monitoring to ascertain adverse event profiles across the dose-escalation cohorts.
