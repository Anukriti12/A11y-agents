Information Security Policy

All endpoints must be hardened against CVEs before deployment to production. The SOC team will perform penetration testing to identify attack vectors. Any PII found in logs must be redacted to ensure GDPR compliance.

Ingress traffic is filtered via WAF rules at the perimeter. Zero-trust architecture means lateral movement is mitigated by microsegmentation. The CISO has final sign-off on all changes to the DMZ topology.
